## Brand & Style
The brand embodies the mindful, disciplined, and deeply affectionate world of positive canine training through a Nordic-inspired lens. It rejects industrial harshness, neon cues, and synthetic visual tropes in favor of quiet craft, tactile warmth, and organic utility. The audience consists of dedicated dog handlers, professional behaviorists, and discerning pet owners seeking calm competence rather than high-stimulus gamification.

The aesthetic fuses **Warm Scandinavian Minimalism** with **Tactile Heritage Craft**. It evokes the touch of hand-stitched saddle leather leads, sunlit birch floorboards, and raw canvas utility bags. Every surface must feel breathable, deliberate, and free of clinical coldness. Under no circumstances should green undertones, sage, olive, or mint tones penetrate the visual language—the earthiness is grounded entirely in baked sienna, rich acorn, golden honeys, and espresso charcoals. The interface balances high legibility for outdoor use with warm editorial poise for long-form training curricula.

## Colors
The palette is built from organic earth elements, deliberately purged of any green wavelength. The primary color is a resonant **Acorn Saddle Brown** (`#7A431D`), offering authoritative warmth for anchor buttons, badges, and primary wayfinding. The secondary color is a luminous **Warm Amber** (`#D9822B`), capturing natural sunlight through treated resin, used for primary highlights, dynamic active states, and callout moments. The tertiary color is **Honey Ochre** (`#E5B25D`), ideal for sub-badges, secondary accents, and warm status indicators.

The neutral backbone anchors in **Espresso Charcoal** (`#231B15`) for high-contrast, fatigue-free typography, entirely replacing harsh synthetic blacks. Backgrounds and working surfaces rest on a stepped progression of **Toasted Wheat** (`#F3E9DC`) and **Warm Sand** (`#FBF7F0`). Surface tiers layer from soft, sunlit canvas up to warm linen cards, maintaining an organic parchment tactile warmth throughout.

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy balances robust craftsmanship with swift field legibility. Headings set in **Epilogue** communicate grounded strength, tactile integrity, and architectural warmth. The proportions feel handcrafted yet disciplined.

Body copy relies on **Source Sans 3** for clear, open counters and humane rhythm across training modules, behavior logs, and tactical guides. Technical markers, timecodes, behavioral metrics, and operational flags utilize **Space Grotesk** in uppercase or semi-condensed configurations to provide precise instrument feel without breaking the earthy atmosphere.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop, scaling down to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile viewports. Spacing is intentionally generous to mimic Scandinavian architectural openness, giving content breathing room and lowering visual cognitive load during high-focus handling sessions.

Vertical pacing adheres to an 8px base rhythm. Outer page margins adapt smoothly: wide 48px (`3rem`) buffers frame desktop editorial training manuals, compressing down to 20px (`1.25rem`) on field-ready mobile interfaces. Component interiors prioritize clean horizontal groupings with tight micro-spacing (`space-xs` and `space-sm`) and relaxed section division (`space-lg` and `space-xl`).

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation abandons cold synthetic box-shadows in favor of **warm tonal layering** and subtle **ambient leather-waxed casting**. Visual hierarchy is communicated primarily through distinct canvas stepping: baseline canvas (`#FBF7F0`), raised cards (`#F3E9DC`), and focused overlays (`#EDE1D0`).

When directional depth is required for floating quick-action buttons or modular panels, cast shadows use low-opacity Espresso Charcoal tinted with amber warmth: `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(35, 27, 21, 0.08), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(35, 27, 21, 0.04)`. Card borders feature whisper-light warm boundaries using semi-translucent saddle pigment (`rgba(122, 67, 29, 0.12)`), establishing physical definition without visual clutter.

## Shapes
The shape language draws inspiration from saddlery, bevelled vegetable-tanned leather pieces, and smooth Scandinavian woodwork. Radius tokens reflect a soft, disciplined curve (`roundedness: 1`), keeping elements structured and sturdy rather than bubbly or toy-like.

Standard containers and buttons use `0.25rem` (4px) corner radiuses. Larger interactive cards and modular lesson blocks employ `rounded-lg` (`0.5rem` / 8px). Hero image containers and drawer sheets cap at `rounded-xl` (`0.75rem` / 12px). Circular geometry is reserved strictly for avatar icons, step badges, and status pips.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Acorn Brown solid fill (`#7A431D`) with high-contrast Sand text (`#FBF7F0`). Hover activates a warm, subtle amber wash (`#633516`). Active state shifts slightly deeper with a compressed inner rim.
- **Secondary:** Warm Sand background (`#FBF7F0`) bound by a 1.5px solid Saddle border (`#7A431D`), text set in `#7A431D`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** No border or fill; uses bold `label-lg` in `#7A431D` with an underline accent visible on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Built with a 4px corner radius. Training category chips utilize Toasted Wheat fill (`#F3E9DC`) with `#231B15` typography.
- Status and achievement chips leverage Honey Ochre (`#E5B25D`) with Espresso Charcoal text for a sunny, tactile reward marker.

### Lists
- Training curricula and behavior history items are structured with hairline dividers in `#EADBC8`.
- Rows include generous vertical padding (`space-md`), featuring numeric or icon index markers set in Space Grotesk.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square controls with 2px corner radius for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: Sand background with a 1.5px border in `#A89887`.
- Checked: Solid Acorn Brown (`#7A431D`) with a warm off-white, hand-drawn style check icon. No cool blues or neon greens allowed.

### Input Fields
- Input surfaces rest on `#FBF7F0` bordered by 1px warm charcoal-tinted leather (`#D5C4B1`).
- Focus rings cast a 2px offset ring in Warm Amber (`#D9822B`), signaling active readiness without distracting the handler.
- Placeholder copy is rendered in muted saddle tone (`#8C7A6B`).

### Cards
- Lesson cards, handler notes, and biometric tracking widgets are framed on `#F3E9DC` with a hairline warm perimeter.
- Subtle inner padding (`space-lg`) organizes content into headline, metadata row, and primary action anchor.

### Specialized Canine Components
- **Bite & Lure Progression Meters:** Segmented linear bars using Honey Ochre and Acorn Brown to visualize drive thresholds and session duration.
- **Field Whistle / Clicker Cue Tags:** Monospaced pill markers (`label-sm`) with high contrast to note audio cues and hand commands clearly under harsh daylight conditions.
